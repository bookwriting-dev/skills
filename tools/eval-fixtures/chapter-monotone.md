# Chapter Two — The Quiet Ledger

She counted the crates along the silent dock. She counted the barrels stacked by the wall. She counted the coins left in her purse. She counted the gulls waiting on the posts. She counted the knots in the mooring line. She counted the lanterns still burning dim. She counted the reasons she still stayed here.

She counted the crates again before the dawn. She counted the same coins in her purse. She counted the same gulls on the posts. She counted the knots her hands already knew. She counted the lanterns the harbor never lit. She counted the reasons and found one less.
