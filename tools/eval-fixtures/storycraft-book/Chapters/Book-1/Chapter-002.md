# Chapter 2 — The Letter

Cold rain found the split in Ren's boot. He curled his toes and knocked again.

“Ain’t yours,” Jo said, pocketing the rent.

Ren showed her the landlord's photograph. Jo glanced at the stairs before she shut the door. The key turned. Too late. Ren heard the bolt slide home.

Outside, Mara waited with a sealed letter. “He left this for me.”

She did not open it. Ren could see the landlord's address on the envelope, but the handwriting meant nothing to him.

“Go now,” she said. Two words were all she trusted herself with.
