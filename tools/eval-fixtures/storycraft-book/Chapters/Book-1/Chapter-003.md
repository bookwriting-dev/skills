# Chapter 3 — The Signature

Mara broke the seal at the canal. Her brother's name was not at the bottom. Jo's was.

Ren read the page twice. Jo had paid the landlord to hide Mara's brother. The photograph was a warning, not proof of death.

“She knew we would come,” he said.

Mara flattened the receipt against the letter. Two payments, one address. She had mistaken the debt for a ransom.

The canal swallowed the moon as a barge passed. Mara started toward Jo's door. This time she would ask a different question.
