# Chapter 1 — The Receipt

“Were you there?” Ren asked.

Mara folded the receipt until its edges met. Her brother's name was printed on the back. She could forgive the lie. It was his relief that she could not bear.

“Not my son,” the landlord had said when she showed him the photograph. Now the rent receipt carried the same address.

Ren reached for the folded paper. Mara put it in her pocket. “Tomorrow.”

He left his cup beside hers and turned out the light. Tomorrow she would tell him.
