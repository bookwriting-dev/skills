- **Passive Skills:** Permanent buffs; take up limited passive slots (e.g. 5 max passives).
- **Skill Progression:** Skills rank from 1 to 10 (or Novice -> Apprentice -> Journeyman -> Master). Proficiency rises through practical use or skill points.

## 4. Fair-Play & "No Free Lunch" Rules

1. **Resource Exhaustion:** If MP reaches 0, caster suffers Mana Burn (headache, disorientation, cannot cast). If SP reaches 0, character collapses from exhaustion.
2. **Stat Realism:** Increasing Strength without Constitution causes self-inflicted muscle damage when hitting hard objects.
3. **No Retcons:** A stat point once committed cannot be reallocated without rare legendary relics.
4. **Anti-Deus Ex Machina:** The system will never award a novel, unforeshadowed instant-kill skill in the middle of a losing fight. All victories must leverage prepared tools, unlocked skills, or tactical exploitation of terrain.
