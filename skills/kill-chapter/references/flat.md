# Pass: kill "flat"

"flat" is correct for **physical flatness** you could see or touch; wrong when stretched
over a **voice, manner, gaze, emotion, or abstract noun** as a mood adjective. In almost
every figurative case the adjective can simply be **removed**.

**KEEP (do not touch) — literal flatness:**
- Surfaces: "the sea went flat," "flat grey water," "the wind had gone flat," "a flat field."
- Posture / placement: "knocked me flat," "stretched flat," "laid out flat," "flat on his back," "hand went flat," "the blade flat against."
- Body language (e.g. animal ears pinned flat) naming a real physical position.
- Hulls / construction: "flat bottom," "flat-bottomed," "flat-floored."
- Objects with a flat plane: "a flat note," "a flat key," "flat spikes," "the flat of the blade."
- "the flat" as a noun (a tidal flat, a plain, the flat of a tool).
- **Quoted dialogue:** keep natural speech (`"a flat voice"`, `"flat broke"`) unless it is clearly repetitive authorial narration padding.
- Idioms: "flat broke," "three days flat," "flat-out," "fell flat."

**FIX (rewrite or cut only where figurative padding weakens narration):**
- narrative modifiers where "flat" is stretched over an abstract/emotional noun: "flat certainty," "the slow flat truth," "flat resolve," "flat dead quiet," "the world: flat, total."
- narrative voice/gaze crutches when overused: "looked at me, flat and unhurried," "a flat clever interest," "flat patience" -> cut or replace with the concrete acoustic/behavioral truth (`"in a level voice"`, `"tonelessly"`, `"without inflection"`), **never** produce broken English like `"in a voice"` or `"he said it"`.
- Repetition: "flat" twice within a few lines where one is figurative.

Only delete `"flat"` outright when the remaining phrase stands cleanly on its own (`"flat certainty"` → `"certainty"`). When modifying a voice or gaze (`"in a flat voice"`), reword to `"in a level voice"` or `"tonelessly"` — or KEEP if spoken in dialogue or isolated. Be conservative on borderline literal cases (a "flat ashen grey" colour, a "flat hard slap" acoustic plane); when genuinely ambiguous, KEEP and note.

Verify grep (check remaining hits in narration only; dialogue and literal uses are valid):
`grep -rniE "flat (certainty|truth|resolve|patience|interest|quiet)" <files>`
