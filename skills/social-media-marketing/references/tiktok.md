# TikTok (BookTok and Non-Fiction Creator Content)

## Technical Specifications

### Video Formats & Resolution
- **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 vertical (required for full-screen immersive playback). 1:1 and 16:9 are technically supported but heavily deprioritized.
- **Recommended Resolution:** 1080 × 1920 px (minimum 540 × 960 px).
- **File Formats:** `.mp4`, `.mov`, `.mpeg`, `.3gp`, `.avi`.
- **File Size Limits:** Up to 500 MB (via Ads Manager); up to 4 GB (native organic web/app upload).
- **Bitrate:** $\ge$ 2,500 kbps recommended for sharp rendering of text and graphics.

### Video Duration
- **Organic Uploads:** Supports up to 10 minutes.
- **Sweet Spot:** 15–35 seconds for quick trope hooks, aesthetic teasers, and reading reactions. Longer formats (45–90s) work well for storytime, thesis breakdowns, or behind-the-scenes writing deep dives.
- **TikTok In-Feed Ads:** 5 to 60 seconds (9–15 seconds is the top-performing sweet spot).

### UI Safe Zones (1080 × 1920 Canvas)
Dynamic UI overlays (username, caption, sound marquee, right-side engagement icons) block content outside the safe zone:
- **Top Margin:** Keep text and faces at least **150–200 px** below the top edge (clears the search bar, sound tag, and Following/For You tabs).
- **Right Margin:** Keep critical text and focal points at least **120–140 px** away from the right edge (clears the profile icon, like, comment, bookmark, and share buttons).
- **Bottom Margin:** Keep subtitles, on-screen text, and book covers at least **250–320 px** above the bottom edge (clears creator handle, caption text, and scrolling audio title).
  - *Dynamic Safe Zone Rule:* If your caption spans 3–4 lines or you run a Spark Ad with a CTA button, the bottom UI expands upward. Place primary captions and subtitles in the center third of the screen ($1080 \times 1280$ px window).
- **Left Margin:** Maintain at least **60 px** of padding from the left edge.

### Captions & Text Limits
- **Organic Native Caption Limit:** Up to **4,000 characters** (including hashtags and @mentions) when posting in the TikTok app. (Restricted to **2,200 characters** if scheduled via third-party posting APIs).
- **Mobile Truncation Fold:** The on-screen caption cuts off after roughly **40–50 characters** before a "See more" link. Put the hook or premise in the first line.
- **In-Feed Ads Caption Limit:** **100 characters max** (strict; no clickable links or hashtags).
- **Spark Ads:** Retain the full organic post's caption and tags.

### Hashtags & Keywords
- **Target Count:** 3–5 tags. Algorithms now rely heavily on in-video spoken audio and on-screen text for SEO; keyword stuffing hurts reach.
- **Structure:**
  - 1 Ultra-niche tag (e.g., `#enemiestolovers`, `#scificyberpunk`, `#habittracker`)
  - 1–2 Community tags (e.g., `#booktok`, `#indieauthor`, `#nonfictionbooks`)
  - 1 Broader genre tag (e.g., `#fantasybooks`, `#productivity`)

## Content Strategy & Formats

### Core Rules
- **Hook in the first 1–3 seconds:** Open directly with a trope, conflict, moral dilemma, or shocking insight. Never open with an author greeting, book title slide, or logo.
- **One idea per video:** One trope combination, one emotional scene beat, one practical framework, or one question.
- **Native aesthetic:** Authentic, creator-shot video outperforms glossy commercial ads. Use native TikTok text styling or clean sans-serif typography within safe zones.
- **Engagement reply loops:** Use video replies to answer reader comments—this generates follow-up content that automatically tags the original video.

### Proven Book Formats

| Format | Fiction (BookTok) | Non-Fiction |
|---|---|---|
| **Trope / "Read this if"** | "If you want grumpy × sunshine with morally grey magic..." | "Read this if you struggle with deep focus after 2 PM..." |
| **Direct Quote / Passage** | Dramatic audio over aesthetic visuals displaying a single high-impact quote | Highlighted passage showing the core counterintuitive thesis |
| **Aesthetic / Mood Board** | Visual montage of setting, atmosphere, and character vibes | Visual overview of the framework, diagram, or workspace |
| **Emotional Reaction** | Author or early reader reacting to chapter climax/twist (spoiler-free) | "The hardest truth I had to admit in chapter 4..." |
| **Behind the Scenes** | Cover design decisions, manuscript drafting progress, proof delivery | Primary research discoveries, failed hypotheses, expert interviews |
| **Myth-Busting / Storytime** | "Why I refused to write a typical chosen-one ending" | "Stop doing X to achieve Y—here is what the data actually says" |

## Cautions & Compliance
- **Account Type:** A Personal Account has access to all trending commercial music; a Business Account is restricted to the Commercial Audio Library but allows bio links from day one without follower thresholds.
- **Content Boundaries & Shadowbans:** Adult romance, dark thriller, or violent horror content must use platform-compliant phrasing (avoid explicit anatomical or violent terms in text overlays and captions).
- **No Spoilers:** Protect plot twists beyond the standard back-cover blurb.
