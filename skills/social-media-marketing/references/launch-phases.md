# Launch Phases and Ongoing Rhythm

## Launch campaign

### Pre-launch (about 4 weeks out to day -1)

Goal: build an audience that is ready to buy or pre-order on day one.

| Week | Focus | Typical assets |
|---|---|---|
| -4 | Announce the book and the hook. Start collecting emails or follows. | Announcement post, "why I wrote this", trope or thesis hook |
| -3 | Cover reveal. Open ARC or reader-team sign-ups. | Teaser crops, full reveal, ARC call |
| -2 | Character, excerpt, or key-idea content. Pre-order push. | Quote cards, excerpt carousels, mood board, pre-order link |
| -1 | Countdown. Reader-team reminders. Launch event set up. | Countdown Stories, event invite, ARC reader posts |

### Launch week (day 0 to day 7)

Goal: concentrate sales and reviews in a short window.

- **Day 0:** launch announcement on all platforms, a pinned post, a Live or Q&A, and a direct thank-you to the reader team.
- **Days 1–3:** reader reactions, shares of early reviews (with permission), and a "where to buy" post.
- **Days 4–7:** a milestone or thank-you post, a giveaway or excerpt, and a gentle review request to readers.

### Post-launch (weeks 1–4 after)

Goal: keep the book visible and turn early readers into word of mouth.

- Share reader-generated content and reviews, with permission.
- Repurpose the best-performing organic content.
- Start the ongoing rhythm below.
- If paid ads are on: begin or scale here, with winning organic posts as creatives.

## Ongoing rhythm (evergreen)

Pick a weekly pattern the author can sustain. A minimal viable version:

| Frequency | Content |
|---|---|
| 2–3 short videos a week | Rotate content pillars (TikTok first, reuse on Reels and Facebook) |
| 1 carousel or quote card a week | Instagram, with a Facebook cross-post |
| 1 community post a week | A question, poll, or reader spotlight (Facebook group or Stories) |
| Monthly | A "what's new" update (new review, price promo, series news, upcoming release) |

Rules:
- Fewer posts done well beat a calendar the author drops after two weeks.
- Rotate pillars so the feed doesn't become all sales.
- Rule of thumb: most content is value, entertainment, or community; a minority is a direct ask.
- Rebuild the calendar every 4 weeks using the retrospective.

## Series and backlist

- A new release in a series reactivates the earlier books: link back to Book 1, and consider a Book 1 promotion alongside the new launch.
- Never spoil earlier-book twists in a later-book campaign.
- Keep one campaign per book; reuse the strategy, not the creative.
