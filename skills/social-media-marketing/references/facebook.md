# Facebook (Pages, Groups, Events, and Reels)

## Technical Specifications

### Formats, Dimensions & Aspect Ratios

| Asset Type | Aspect Ratio | Optimal Resolution | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Feed Image (Portrait)** | 4:5 vertical *(recommended)* | 1080 × 1350 px | Takes up maximum vertical real estate in mobile feeds. |
| **Feed Image (Square)** | 1:1 square | 1080 × 1080 px | Clean for cross-posting and desktop. |
| **Link Preview Image (OG Card)** | 1.91:1 landscape | 1200 × 630 px | Minimum 600 × 315 px. Controls preview image when sharing retail/blog links. |
| **Facebook Reels** | 9:16 vertical | 1080 × 1920 px | Max duration: 90 seconds. Cross-posts seamlessly from Instagram Reels. |
| **Stories** | 9:16 vertical | 1080 × 1920 px | Max duration: 60 seconds per video card. |
| **Page / Group Cover Photo** | ~2.7:1 landscape | 851 × 315 px | Displayed at 820 × 312 px on desktop and 640 × 360 px on mobile. Keep text centered within the central 640 × 312 px area to avoid mobile crop. |
| **Profile Photo** | 1:1 circle crop | 720 × 720 px (or 1080 × 1080 px) | Minimum 320 × 320 px. Renders as a circle. |

### Post Copy & Text Truncation
- **Maximum Character Limit:** Up to **63,206 characters** per standard organic post.
- **Feed Truncation Fold:** Mobile posts truncate with "See More" between **125 and 250 characters** depending on spacing and line breaks. The primary hook and premise must sit before the fold.
- **Link Previews:** Direct URL shares automatically generate a link card pulled from Open Graph metadata (`<meta property="og:image" content="...">`). If the image is smaller than 600 × 315 px, Facebook displays it as a tiny square thumbnail instead of a full-width card.

## Strategic Channels

### 1. Author Page (Official Home Base)
- **Role:** Central hub for discovery, official announcements, pinned retail links, and Meta advertising compliance.
- **Reach Reality:** Organic reach from business/author pages is algorithmically restricted (often 2–5% of followers). Pages serve as authority anchors and advertising accounts rather than viral engines.
- **Best Practices:** Pin the current book's launch post or universal book link (UBL) at the top of the page. Post 2–3 times a week with high-value excerpts, cover reveals, or behind-the-scenes milestones.

### 2. Facebook Groups (High-Conversion Community)
- **Author's Own Group ("Street Team" / "ARC Team"):** The highest converting organic asset for indie authors. Use it to coordinate advance reviews, reveal draft snippets, vote on bonus scenes, and mobilize launch-week purchases.
- **Genre & Book Club Groups:** Readers in Romance, Thriller, Cozy Mystery, Sci-Fi, and Non-Fiction gather in active groups.
  - *Strict Rule:* Never spam cold promotional links. Participate organically; only share links on designated "Self-Promotion" weekly threads or when readers explicitly ask for recommendations matching your comp titles.

### 3. Facebook Events
- Create an official **Book Launch Event** 2–3 weeks prior to release.
- Invites and RSVP notifications generate reminders directly on users' personal notification feeds on launch day.

## Compliance & Best Practices
- **Giveaways & Contests:** Meta's Terms of Service strictly forbid making personal timeline shares or tagging friends a mandatory condition of entry ("Share on your timeline to enter" violates promotion guidelines). Contests must state that the promotion is in no way sponsored, endorsed, or administered by Facebook.
- **Review Integrity:** Never organize review swaps or incentivize positive reviews. Request honest, voluntary reviews.
- **No Link Spam:** Repeatedly copy-pasting the exact same Amazon or retailer link across multiple groups or pages will trigger Facebook's automated spam filters and flag your domain.
