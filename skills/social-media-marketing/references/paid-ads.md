# Paid Advertising (Optional Layer)

Paid ads are **off by default**. Use this file only when the user explicitly requests paid ad campaigns.

---

## Technical Specifications & Copy Limits

### Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram Feed, Stories, Reels)

#### Copy Field Constraints
- **Primary Text:**
  - *Recommended Length:* **125 characters** (or 1–2 short sentences) to prevent mobile truncation.
  - *Technical Maximum:* Supports up to 2,200+ characters, but everything after ~125 characters is hidden behind a *"See More"* toggle.
  - *Best Practice:* Put the hook, trope combination, or reader problem within the first 120 characters.
- **Headline:**
  - *Recommended Length:* **27 to 40 characters** (40 general, 27 for Facebook Feed to prevent mid-word truncation).
  - *Best Practice:* Focus strictly on the primary hook or offer (e.g., "Free on Kindle Unlimited", "The Year's Darkest Fantasy").
- **Description:**
  - *Recommended Length:* **20 to 30 characters**.
  - *Placement Note:* Sits below the headline on select desktop and mobile feed placements; often hidden on smaller mobile screens. Treat it as secondary reinforcement (e.g., "Over 5,000 5-Star Reviews").
- **Multiple Text Options (Dynamic Creative):**
  - Meta allows inputting up to **5 Primary Texts, 5 Headlines, and 5 Descriptions** per ad. Meta's delivery engine dynamically tests combinations to optimize delivery per viewer.

#### Creative Dimensions & Asset Requirements
- **Feed (Facebook & Instagram):** 1:1 square (1080 × 1080 px) or 4:5 portrait (1080 × 1350 px). 4:5 is strongly recommended for mobile feed takeovers.
- **Reels & Stories:** 9:16 vertical (1080 × 1920 px). Adhere to safe zones (top 250 px, bottom 420 px clear of UI overlays).
- **Video Requirements:** MP4 or MOV, H.264 compression, max 4 GB. Keep video under 15–30 seconds for feed ads.

---

### TikTok Ads (In-Feed & Spark Ads)

#### Copy & Ad Constraints
- **In-Feed Ad Text:** **100 characters maximum** (including punctuation and spaces). Truncates after roughly 40–50 characters on standard mobile screens. No clickable hashtags or external links in standard ad text.
- **Spark Ads (Recommended for Authors):**
  - Uses an authorization code generated from an organic TikTok video.
  - Retains the organic post's full caption (up to 4,000 characters), comments, likes, and native audio.
  - The CTA button links directly to your retailer or landing page while driving traffic and engagement directly to the author's TikTok profile.

#### Creative Dimensions
- **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 vertical (1080 × 1920 px).
- **Duration:** 5 to 60 seconds (optimal performance is **9–15 seconds**).
- **Safe Zones:** Crucial to keep text away from top 150–200 px and bottom 250–320 px (where the clickable CTA anchor button appears).

## When to Run Ads
1. **Validation First:** Do not run paid ads on unproven creative. Take an organic video, quote card, or carousel that achieved above-average saves, shares, or click-throughs, and convert it into an ad.
2. **Landing Page Ready:** Retailer buy page, pre-order page, or email opt-in page must be tested and functional.
3. **Budget Feasibility:** Start with a test budget ($5–$10/day per ad set) that can run for 4–7 days without requiring immediate profitability.

---

## Campaign Structure & Testing Framework

### Phase 1: Creative & Hook Testing (Low Budget)
- **Objective:** Traffic (Link Clicks) or Leads (Email Signups).
- **Structure:** 1 Campaign $\rightarrow$ 2–3 Ad Sets (different audiences) $\rightarrow$ 2–3 Creatives per ad set.
- **Audience Buckets:**
  - *Broad Genre / Comp Authors:* Readers who follow popular authors with similar tropes/styles.
  - *Category Interests:* Specific genres (e.g., "Dark Romance", "Cyberpunk", "Personal Finance").
  - *Custom Retargeting:* Page engagers, video viewers ($\ge 50\%$), or website visitors.
- **Decision Rule:** Run for 4–7 days until each ad set reaches at least 500–1,000 impressions.
  - Pause ads with high Cost-Per-Click (CPC) or low Click-Through-Rate (CTR $< 1.0\%$).
  - Keep and scale creatives with strong CTR ($\ge 1.5–2.5\%$) and low CPC.

### Phase 2: Launch or Conversion Scaling
- Shift budget toward winning creatives and proven audiences.
- For books in a series: Calculate profitability based on **Read-Through Value** (Book 1 sales + subsequent book purchases), not solely Book 1 immediate revenue.

## Compliance & Advertising Policies
- **Claims & Guarantees:** Non-fiction ads must never guarantee specific financial, health, or lifestyle outcomes.
- **Restricted / Sensitive Categories:** If the book touches political history, social issues, or financial topics, declare the Special Ad Category if Meta prompts for it.
- **Adult Content Policies:** Meta and TikTok strictly forbid sexually suggestive imagery, exposed skin beyond standard artistic thresholds, or explicit sexual copy. Romance and dark fiction ads must focus on emotional tropes, tension quotes, and aesthetic covers rather than explicit content.
- **Testimonial Verification:** Reviews and editorial quotes featured in ads must be genuine. Do not alter review wording or fabricate praise.
